What We Do

Complete Water Infrastructure Services

DSP Drilling & Constructions delivers every stage of your borehole and water system — from first drill to final flow test.

Borehole Drilling

Access clean, reliable water — anywhere. Full-depth rotary drilling suited to South African geology.

Pump Installations

Powerful performance, reliable supply. Submersible pumps sized correctly to your borehole's yield.

PVC Installations

Strong, durable, leak-free piping for delivery lines, reticulation and irrigation networks.

Casing & Screen Installation

For clean, protected boreholes — certified casing that safeguards structural integrity long-term.

Test Pumping & Water Quality Checks

Ensuring safe, sustainable water — accurate yield testing and quality verification before hand-over.

Maintenance & Repairs

Keep your water system flowing with inspections, servicing and prompt repairs when you need them.

Flushing Of Boreholes

Sealing and protection for long life — clearing sediment to keep your borehole performing at its best.
